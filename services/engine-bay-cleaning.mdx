A clean engine bay isn't just about looks — it makes it easier to spot leaks, keeps components running cooler, and gives your vehicle a well-maintained appearance under the hood. R15's engine bay cleaning service safely degrease and cleans your engine compartment without damaging sensitive components.

## What's Included

- Covering of sensitive electrical components before any cleaning begins
- Application of professional degreaser to break down grime and oil buildup
- Low-pressure rinse (no high-pressure water near electronics)
- Hand agitation on stubborn areas
- Drying and dressing of hoses, covers, and plastic components
- Final inspection

## Is It Safe?

Yes — engine bay cleaning is safe when done correctly. R15 protects electrical components, sensors, and air intakes before any water or product is applied. We use low-pressure rinsing and take care around vulnerable areas.

<Warning>
  We do not recommend engine bay cleaning on vehicles with known electrical issues or damaged wiring. If you're unsure, mention it when requesting your quote and we can advise.
</Warning>

**Approximate time:** ~30–45 minutes

## Ideal For

- Vehicles with visible grease or oil buildup
- Pre-sale preparation (a clean engine bay impresses buyers)
- General maintenance and inspection prep
