The Interior Essential focuses entirely on the inside of your vehicle. It's a deep, thorough clean of every surface, fabric, and crevice — the kind of clean that makes you feel like you're driving a new car. From shampooed carpets to conditioned door panels, we leave no corner untouched.

## What's Included

- Full interior vacuum (seats, floors, trunk/cargo, under seats)
- Carpet and floor mat shampoo and extraction
- Seat cleaning (fabric shampoo or leather wipe-down depending on material)
- Dashboard, console, and door panel deep clean and conditioning
- Interior window cleaning
- Air vent cleaning
- Cup holder and storage compartment detail
- Headliner spot cleaning

## Ideal For

- Vehicles with stained or dirty carpets and seats
- Musty or odor-affected interiors
- Pre-sale or post-purchase cleaning
- Regular deep maintenance

**Approximate time:** ~1.5–2 hours
