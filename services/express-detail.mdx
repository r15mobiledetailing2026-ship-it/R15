The Express Detail is R15's quick-turnaround service for customers who want their vehicle looking sharp without the time commitment of a full detail. In roughly one hour, your car gets a thorough exterior wash and a basic interior tidy — leaving it clean, fresh, and road-ready with minimal disruption to your day.

## What's Included

- Hand wash and rinse of all exterior surfaces
- Wheel and tire cleaning
- Window cleaning (exterior)
- Interior vacuum (seats, floors, cargo area)
- Dashboard and console wipe-down
- Door jamb wipe

## Ideal For

- Regular maintenance between full details
- Light dust or surface dirt
- Vehicles in generally good condition that just need a refresh

**Approximate time:** ~1 hour
