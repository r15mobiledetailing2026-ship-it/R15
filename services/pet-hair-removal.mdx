Pet hair is one of the most stubborn detailing challenges. It embeds itself deep into carpet fibers and fabric seats and doesn't come out with a regular vacuum. R15's pet hair removal service uses specialized tools and techniques to extract fur thoroughly — leaving your interior clean and fresh.

## What's Included

- Pre-treatment to loosen embedded hair from fabric
- Specialized rubber brush and lint tool agitation on all fabric surfaces
- High-powered vacuum extraction
- Seat and carpet cleaning after hair removal
- Final vacuum pass and wipe-down

## What to Expect

The amount of time and effort required depends heavily on the amount of pet hair, the vehicle size, and whether hair is in seats, carpets, or both. Some vehicles with heavy accumulation may require multiple passes.

**Approximate time:** ~1–2 hours depending on severity

  If your vehicle has heavy pet hair buildup, we recommend combining this service with the [Interior Essential](/services/interior-essential) for a complete deep clean of all interior surfaces.
</Note>
