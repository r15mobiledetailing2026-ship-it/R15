Ceramic Spray Protection is a professional-grade paint protection treatment that bonds to your vehicle's clear coat, creating a hydrophobic barrier that repels water, dirt, and UV rays. It's an excellent upgrade after any exterior detail — keeping your car looking freshly washed long after you've driven it off the lot.

## How It Works

After your paint is clean and decontaminated, we apply a ceramic-infused spray polymer that bonds to the surface. The result is a high-gloss, slick finish that keeps your car cleaner for longer and makes future washing easier.

## Benefits

- **Hydrophobic effect** — water beads and rolls off the surface
- **UV protection** — slows paint fading and oxidation
- **Enhanced gloss and depth of color**
- **Easier to wash** — dirt and grime don't stick as aggressively
- **Protection lasting several months** (varies by climate and washing habits)

## What It Includes

- Application of ceramic spray to all painted exterior surfaces
- Buff to a high-gloss finish
- Tire and trim dressing

**Approximate time:** ~30 minutes as an add-on to an existing service
