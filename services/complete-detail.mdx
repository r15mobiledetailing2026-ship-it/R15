The Complete Detail is R15's most comprehensive service. It combines a thorough interior deep clean with a meticulous exterior detail, bringing your vehicle to showroom condition from bumper to bumper. This is the service our customers rave about.

## What's Included

### Interior

- Full vacuum (seats, carpets, trunk, under seats)
- Carpet and floor mat shampoo and extraction
- Seat cleaning (fabric shampoo or leather conditioning)
- Dashboard, console, and trim deep clean and dressing
- Door panel and door jamb detail
- Interior glass cleaning
- Air vent cleaning
- Cup holder and storage detail

### Exterior

- Hand wash with premium soap
- Clay bar decontamination (removes bonded contaminants)
- Wheel and tire scrub and dressing
- Exterior glass cleaning
- Door jamb wipe
- Paint surface wipe-down and finishing spray
- Trim dressing

**Approximate time:** ~3–5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition

## Ideal For

- Vehicles needing a full reset
- Pre-sale preparation
- Post-winter restoration
- Special occasions (wedding cars, gifts, etc.)
