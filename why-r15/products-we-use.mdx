The quality of a detail is only as good as the products used to do it. At R15, we use professional-grade detailing products — not the bargain-bin consumer stuff. The products we choose are safe for your vehicle's surfaces, effective at removing contaminants, and designed to produce lasting results.

## Why Product Quality Matters

Consumer-grade products from auto parts stores are formulated for convenience, not performance. Professional products use higher-grade surfactants, conditioners, and protectants that do a better job, go further, and are safer on sensitive surfaces like leather, clear coats, and painted trim.

## Product Categories We Use

- **pH-Neutral Car Wash Soaps** — Cleans thoroughly without stripping wax or paint protection
- **Clay Bar Systems** — Removes bonded surface contaminants like rail dust, tree sap, and overspray
- **Interior Cleaners & Dressings** — Surface-specific formulas for plastic, vinyl, leather, and fabric
- **Carpet & Upholstery Shampoo** — Professional hot-water extraction-grade cleaners for deep stain removal
- **Glass Cleaners** — Streak-free formulas for crystal-clear windows inside and out
- **Ceramic Spray Polymer** — Professional-grade, SiO2-infused spray coating for paint protection
- **Tire & Trim Dressings** — Long-lasting matte or gloss finish products
- **Engine Degreasers** — Heavy-duty, safe-on-contact formulas for engine bay cleaning

<Note>
  We carefully select products that are safe for all vehicle surface types including factory paint, aftermarket wraps, chrome, and leather. If your vehicle has a special coating or a wrap, let us know when you book.
</Note>
