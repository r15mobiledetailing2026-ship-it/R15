On the day of your appointment, your R15 detailer arrives ready to work with all the supplies and equipment needed. Here's a walk-through of what the experience looks like from start to finish.

## Your Appointment, Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrival">
    Your detailer arrives at the scheduled time at your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked. They'll introduce themselves and do a quick walk-around of the vehicle with you to note any specific areas of concern.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-Detail Inspection">
    We document the vehicle's current condition and confirm the services to be performed. This is a great time to point out anything specific — a stain, a scratch, a smelly area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Detail">
    We get to work. You're free to go about your day while we take care of your vehicle. You don't need to be present the entire time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final Inspection">
    Once we've completed the service, we'll walk you through the results. We want you to be completely happy with the outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Satisfaction Guaranteed">
    If something isn't quite right, tell us and we'll address it before we leave.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Need to Provide

We bring everything required to do the job — all we ask from you is a little space and access:

- A safely parked vehicle with room to work around it
- Access to an outdoor water spigot is helpful (we carry our own supply for many jobs but it helps for longer services)
- Keys for interior services

## Payment

Payment is due at time of service. We accept cash and most major payment methods — confirm at time of booking.

<Tip>
  You don't have to babysit us! Most customers simply hand us the keys and check back when we're done.
</Tip>
