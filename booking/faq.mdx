Have questions about R15 Mobile Detailing? Here are answers to what customers ask us most often. If you don't see your question here, call or text **518-888-6109**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does detailing cost?">
    Pricing depends on the service, your vehicle size, and condition. An express detail on a compact car starts lower than a complete detail on a large SUV. We provide free, no-obligation quotes — call **518-888-6109** or [request a quote](/booking/get-a-quote).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to be home during the appointment?">
    No! Many customers simply leave their keys with us or arrange access before they leave. You just need to make sure the vehicle is accessible and there's a safe place to park and work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you bring your own water and supplies?">
    Yes, we come fully equipped with professional-grade products and supplies. For longer services, access to a water spigot is helpful, but we carry our own supply for most jobs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What areas do you serve?">
    We're based in Sodus, NY and serve Wayne County and surrounding communities including Sodus Point, Wolcott, Williamson, Newark, Clyde, Lyons, and Macedon. Not sure if we cover your area? [Check our service area](/service-area) or call us.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a detail take?">
    It varies by service:

    - **Express Detail** — ~1 hour
    - **Interior Essential** — ~1.5–2 hours
    - **Complete Detail** — ~3–5 hours
    - **Pet Hair Removal** — ~1–2 hours
    - **Engine Bay** — ~30–45 minutes

    Larger or heavily soiled vehicles may take longer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are you insured?">
    Yes, R15 Mobile Detailing is fully insured. Your vehicle is protected while in our care.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I'm not satisfied with the results?">
    We stand behind our work. If you're not satisfied, tell us before we leave and we'll make it right. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I combine services?">
    Absolutely. Services like Ceramic Spray Protection and Pet Hair Removal can be added to any detail package. Just mention it when requesting your quote.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you detail trucks and SUVs?">
    Yes, we detail all personal vehicles including cars, trucks, SUVs, and minivans. Larger vehicles are priced accordingly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often should I get my car detailed?">
    For most drivers, a full detail once or twice a year with express details in between is ideal. If you have kids or pets, more frequent interior cleanings are worthwhile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
