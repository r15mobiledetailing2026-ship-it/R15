Getting started with R15 Mobile Detailing is easy. Request a free, no-obligation quote and we'll provide transparent pricing based on your vehicle type, location, and the services you need.

## How to Request a Quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call or Text">
    The fastest way to reach us is by phone. Call or text **518-888-6109** with your vehicle make/model, your location, and the service(s) you're interested in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Us About Your Vehicle">
    We'll ask a few quick questions: vehicle type (car, truck, SUV, van), approximate condition (light, moderate, or heavy soiling), and any specific concerns (pet hair, stains, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Quote">
    We'll give you a clear price estimate. No hidden fees, no surprises. If you're happy with the quote, we'll schedule a convenient time to come to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Have Ready

Before you reach out, it helps to have the following on hand:

- Your vehicle's year, make, and model
- Your general location or zip code
- The service(s) you're interested in (or just describe the problem and we'll recommend one)
- Any specific problem areas (stains, pet hair, heavy dirt, etc.)

<Note>
  **Pricing varies by vehicle size and condition.** An SUV or minivan will typically be priced higher than a compact car. Heavily soiled vehicles may require additional time and cost.
</Note>
