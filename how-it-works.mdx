Getting your car detailed with R15 is refreshingly simple. There's no need to drop your vehicle off, rearrange your schedule, or wait at a shop — we bring everything to you. Whether you're at home, at work, or anywhere in between, professional detailing comes to your door in just three easy steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a Quote">
    Call us at **518-888-6109** or fill out the online quote form. Tell us your vehicle type, your location, and which service you're interested in. We'll get back to you quickly with transparent pricing and availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We Come to You">
    Schedule a time that works for your day. Our detailer arrives at your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked — fully equipped with everything needed to get the job done right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy the Results">
    We complete your detail to showroom-quality standards. Take a moment to inspect the results, and let us know if anything needs attention — your satisfaction is guaranteed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You'll Need

We handle all the hard parts. On your end, just make sure you have:

- Access to the vehicle (keys aren't always needed for exterior-only services)
- A parking spot with some room around it
- Access to an outdoor water source is helpful but not always required — we carry our own water for many jobs
